CASE FILE 005

Consumer Growth Strategy

Turning social observations into a measurable consumer journey.

A strategic analysis of two very different consumer categories — fashion/lifestyle and healthcare — using the supplied Instagram profile and feed screenshots. The frameworks below are strategy hypotheses, not claimed platform-reported results.

Fashion · Lifestyle · Consumer Growth

Masaki Couture & Café

Instagram profile + supplied feed screenshots
Instagram ↗
Consumer Insight

Occasion-wear shoppers need inspiration, confidence and social proof before choosing a fashion destination.

Audience

Style-conscious women and young professionals, occasion/festive shoppers, existing customers, and local lifestyle audiences attracted by the fashion + café combination.

Pain Points

Finding the right look for an occasion, trusting quality/design, and understanding why the brand is different from other local options.

Consumer Journey
Discover→Explore→Desire→ Evaluate→Visit / Purchase→Experience →Share→Repeat
Content System
Occasion Styling Design Story Product Detail Social Proof Festive / Seasonal Drops Café + Lifestyle Sales / Offers

Growth Experiments

Styling vs product-first Measure: saves · shares · profile visits
Craftsmanship vs final-look Measure: shares · saves · enquiries
Social proof vs promotional CTA Measure: profile visits · purchase enquiries
30-DAY SYSTEM
Discovery → Consideration → Trust / Community → Conversion / Retention
MEASURE Reach → Saves / Shares → Profile Visits → DMs / Enquiries → Purchase / Visit → Repeat / UGC
Healthcare · Women’s Health · Fertility

Sunrise Hospital

Instagram profile + supplied feed screenshots
Instagram ↗
Consumer Insight

Healthcare audiences need clarity, credibility and reassurance before taking a high-consideration next step.

Audience

Women researching fertility, pregnancy and gynae health; couples evaluating fertility providers; and audiences seeking trustworthy health information.

Pain Points

Knowing what is normal, when to seek help, whether a provider is trustworthy, and what the next step looks like.

Consumer Journey
Question→Awareness→Education→ Trust→Evaluation→Consultation →Appointment→Care→Advocacy
Content System
Fertility Education Condition Awareness Doctor Authority Patient Proof Treatment Education Consultation Guidance Myth / FAQ

Growth Experiments

Question hook vs statement hook Measure: retention · saves · shares
Doctor-led vs graphic-led Measure: engagement quality · profile visits · consultation intent
Education CTA vs appointment CTA Measure: saves / shares · profile visits · enquiries
30-DAY SYSTEM
Awareness → Authority → Trust → Conversion
MEASURE Reach → Watch Time / Retention → Saves / Shares → Profile Visits → DMs / Calls → Consultation Leads → Appointments

Cross-Case Strategy Framework

One framework, adapted to different consumer decision cycles.
Masaki

Emotional and desire-led. Visual discovery and social proof can move audiences toward a lower-friction visit or purchase.

Sunrise

Trust and information-led. Education, authority and reassurance support a higher-consideration path toward consultation.

Common System

Consumer Insight → Persona → Journey → Content Hypothesis → Creative → Distribution → KPI → Experiment → Optimisation → Conversion.
